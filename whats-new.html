DNS 2027 · Unlocking Innovation

The Summit keeps growing.

Every year, DNS becomes a little more than the week in Switzerland. 2027 is our most immersive year yet — a new global prize, six innovation tracks, a companion accessibility app, and expanded programming in Chicago, all part of a platform that now runs year-round.

New for 2027

The Neurodiversity Innovation Prize.

A new global award for a startup, founder, or neurodiverse team using neurodiverse insight to improve work, learning, health, accessibility, AI, design, or quality of life.

Prize Package

What the winner receives

  • The award itself, presented on the Davos stage
  • A featured profile on the DNS Leadership Wall
  • Media assets and storytelling support

Sponsor Package

What a naming partner receives

  • Naming rights to the Prize
  • A seat on the judging panel
  • Direct access to founders and pilot pathways
