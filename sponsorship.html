The Full Picture

What partnership actually includes.

Expand any section for the fuller detail behind it.

Who's in the room

Cross-sector, cross-generational, and cross-neurotype by design — not by accident.

+

Our in-person Davos audience skews toward senior leadership: C-suite executives, SVPs and VPs, entrepreneurs, investors, researchers, policymakers, and technology leaders. In 2026, we saw particularly strong representation from financial services, technology, consulting, and venture capital.

The virtual audience is intentionally broader — roughly a third corporate and investment leaders, a third education, research, and policy leaders, and a third emerging talent and the wider neurodiversity community, including students and young professionals looking for mentors and pathways. The mix is the point: a financial-services leader in conversation with a university president, a venture capitalist with a neurodivergent founder, an established leader with someone just starting out.

Speaking & stage access

Guaranteed at our top three tiers — earned everywhere else.

+

Founding, Presenting, and Platinum partners receive guaranteed speaking roles, with prominence scaling by tier. This works because organizations making that level of commitment typically already have real leadership to contribute on neurodiversity, talent, and the future of work.

Most of our panelists, award recipients, and Leadership Wall honorees are personally selected because we believe they have something important to say — DNS is not a pay-to-play stage. Only a minority of our speakers are sponsors, and that mix is core to the Summit's credibility.

The Innovation Exhibit

Where emerging founders, investors, and corporate partners actually meet.

+

2027 brings a significantly expanded Innovation Exhibit, with several venture capital partners already committed to participate. Founding and Presenting partners are prominently featured in connection with the Exhibit.

The goal is a real point of intersection — neurodivergent founders and talent, investors with capital to deploy, and corporate leaders looking for what's next, in the same room over the course of the week.

Year-round platform

Davos is the convening point — the relationships and content continue for the rest of the year.

+

DNS extends well past the week in Switzerland: a growing media and podcast presence, the DNS Leadership Wall (moving toward an immersive LED format with a life beyond Davos), and a forthcoming book built from conversations that originate at the Summit.

Our virtual community stays engaged long after WEF week — we've seen email open rates approaching 40% within the first 15 minutes of a send, which reflects a community that follows the ideas and people, not just the event.

Logistics & access

Held during WEF Annual Meeting week, with formats ranging from the main stage to twelve-person dinners.

+

DNS 2027 runs in Davos during WEF Annual Meeting week, deliberately scheduled so participants can move between DNS and the broader WEF ecosystem. Top-tier partners select their preferred table placement and build an executive delegation with us directly, rather than purchasing individual seats.

Alongside the principal programming, we intentionally preserve smaller formats — workshops capped near 15–20 people, and select dinners and conversations closer to 12 — because that intimacy is where some of the most valuable relationships actually form.

World Autism Day — April 2

A second touchpoint outside Davos, built around a Nimoy Knight Foundation film on Leonard Nimoy's life.

+

Sponsors also have the opportunity to be represented at our World Autism Day program in Chicago, centered on a film about Leonard Nimoy and conversations about neurodiversity, culture, and leadership — Spock and the Vulcans have long held particular resonance within the autism community.

Scope of involvement depends on partnership level, from brand presence to a role in the on-stage conversation itself.
