About the Summit

A coalition built for inclusive progress.

The story behind DNS, and the founder who convened it during World Economic Forum week.

Our Purpose

A unique perspective at the World Economic Forum.

The Davos Neurodiversity Summit (DNS) provides a distinct perspective during the World Economic Forum, representing the diverse neurominority global community. Through engaging discussions, DNS advocates for inclusivity and innovation that leverages the creativity and resilience of neurodistinct individuals.

Who We Are

A global coalition dedicated to creating inclusive progress.

DNS is a coalition of neurodistinct leaders and influencers that convenes annually during the World Economic Forum to amplify the many voices of the neurodiversity community, representing nearly 1 in 5 people worldwide. The mission of DNS is to bring a unique and vital perspective to the forum's major themes, offering insights shaped by diverse ways of thinking and problem-solving.

By engaging in discussion and commentary, DNS works to ensure that global strategies for economic, social, and technological progress are inclusive and equitable — reflecting the strengths and needs of a neurodiverse world. Through its contributions, the coalition challenges traditional paradigms, advocating for innovation that values and leverages the inherent creativity and resilience within the neurodivergent population.

Founder

Founded by Dr. Maureen Dunne

Dr. Maureen Dunne

Founder, Davos Neurodiversity Summit

Dr. Dunne founded the Davos Neurodiversity Summit during World Economic Forum week, seeing the need to empower global leaders to shape the future WEF agenda by including neurodivergent voices. She is the author of the international bestselling and award-winning book The Neurodiversity Edge.

Dr. Dunne is also a global keynote speaker and leader who has served as a senior adviser and lead trainer to some of the world's top brands, Fortune 500 companies, educational institutions, philanthropic foundations, funds, and government officials. Her work has been featured in outlets including Armchair Expert with Dax Shepard, Harper's Bazaar, MIT Sloan Management Review, Fast Company, Inside Higher Ed, Forbes, Bloomberg, and Big Think. She earned her doctorate from the University of Oxford as a Rhodes Scholar.
